# Self Care Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

Self Care is designed to keep wellbeing information private and under the user’s control.

## Data stored on the device

Mood check-ins, daily factor values, appearance choices, and app preferences are stored locally on the user’s iPhone. Self Care has no account system, advertising, analytics, or developer-operated server.

## Apple Health

With permission, Self Care reads Exercise Time and Sleep Analysis from Apple Health and can save a logged mood as a State of Mind entry. Health access is optional and controlled by the user. Self Care does not send Health data to its developers or servers and does not use it for advertising, marketing, profiling, or data mining. A CSV export may contain factor values read from Apple Health and is shared only when the user chooses a destination.

## Export and deletion

Self Care exports check-in history only when the user chooses Export and selects a destination. Reset Self Care Data removes the app’s local history and preferences and asks HealthKit to remove State of Mind entries Self Care previously saved. If HealthKit can’t remove those entries, Self Care keeps the local data and reports the problem. Health permissions remain managed by iOS.

## Wellbeing, not medical care

Self Care supports personal reflection. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any medical condition and is not a substitute for professional care.
