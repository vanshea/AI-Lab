# Self Care Privacy Policy

Effective July 22, 2026

Self Care is designed to keep wellbeing information private and under the user’s control.

## Data stored on the device

Mood check-ins, daily factor values, appearance choices, and app preferences are stored locally on the user’s iPhone. Self Care has no account system, advertising, analytics, or developer-operated server.

## Apple Health

With permission, Self Care reads Exercise Time and Sleep Analysis from Apple Health and can save a logged mood as a State of Mind entry. Health access is optional and controlled by the user. Self Care does not use Health data for advertising, marketing, profiling, or data mining and does not sell or disclose Health data to third parties.

## Export and deletion

Self Care exports check-in history only when the user chooses Export and selects a destination. Reset Self Care Data removes the app’s local history and settings. Reset does not remove State of Mind entries previously saved in Apple Health; users manage those entries in the Health app.

## Wellbeing, not medical care

Self Care supports personal reflection. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any medical condition and is not a substitute for professional care.
