# Self Care Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 19, 2026

**Last updated:** October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Self Care iOS app (the “App”) handles information. Self Care is designed so that the developer does not collect or receive your personal information. The App processes and stores your check-in information on your device, except when you choose to interact with Apple Health or export information.

## 1. Information handled by the App

Self Care may handle the following information on your device:

- Mood values, mood labels, and the date and time of a check-in.
- Daily factor values, including exercise minutes, sleep hours, and manually entered scrolling time.
- Completed goals, selected categories, and names of custom categories you create.
- App settings such as appearance, reminder preference and time, Apple Health connection preferences, and mood-export preference.
- Technical records needed to keep a Self Care check-in synchronized with a State of Mind entry that the App writes to Apple Health.

This information is stored in the App’s local storage on your iPhone. Self Care does not require an account and does not operate a server that receives this information.

## 2. Apple Health

Apple Health access is optional. Self Care remains usable if you decline Health access.

With your permission, Self Care may:

- Read Apple Exercise Time for the current day.
- Read Sleep Analysis for the relevant overnight period.
- Save your logged mood to Apple Health as a State of Mind entry when you separately enable mood export.
- Add State of Mind associations derived from completed check-in factors or categories, such as fitness, health, family, friends, partner, work, self care, or spirituality, when a category has a meaningful Apple Health association.
- Delete State of Mind entries that Self Care previously created when you delete the related check-in or reset App data.

Health information is used only to support your check-in and the Apple Health features you choose. Self Care does not send Health information to the developer, does not sell it, and does not use it for advertising, marketing, profiling, eligibility decisions, or data mining.

Apple controls Health permissions. You can review or revoke them in **Settings > Privacy & Security > Health > Self Care** or in the Health app. Revoking permission stops future access but does not automatically delete existing Self Care records or State of Mind entries already stored in Apple Health.

## 3. Notifications

If you enable a daily check-in reminder, Self Care stores your reminder preference and selected local time on your device. iOS schedules and delivers the notification. Self Care does not use a remote push-notification server and does not receive a device token.

You can change notification access in **Settings > Notifications > Self Care**.

## 4. Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Self Care contains no developer-operated analytics, advertising, cross-app tracking, or profiling tools. It does not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. Self Care does not collect device identifiers, precise location, contacts, photos, camera recordings, or microphone recordings.

Apple may independently process App Store activity, purchase or download information, and optional device diagnostics under Apple’s own policies and your Apple settings. That processing is controlled by Apple, not by Self Care.

## 5. Exporting and sharing

Self Care creates a CSV export only when you choose **Export history as CSV**. The export may include check-in dates, mood values and labels, exercise minutes, sleep hours, scrolling minutes, completed-goal counts, and Apple Health synchronization status.

The iOS share sheet lets you choose the destination. Self Care does not automatically upload the export. Once you send or save an export to another app, service, person, or location, that destination’s privacy practices apply.

## 6. Retention and deletion

Local check-ins and settings remain on your device until you delete an individual check-in, reset Self Care data, or uninstall the App.

- Deleting an individual check-in removes its local record and asks Apple Health to delete the related Self Care-authored State of Mind entry, if one exists. If the Health entry cannot be removed, the App keeps the local check-in and reports the problem.
- **Reset Self Care data** removes local check-ins, custom categories, and preferences after Self Care-authored State of Mind entries have been removed successfully. Apple Health permissions remain under iOS control.
- Uninstalling Self Care removes its local App data from the device, subject to Apple’s operating-system and backup behavior. Uninstalling does not necessarily delete State of Mind entries already saved in Apple Health; you can manage those entries in the Health app.

Depending on your iPhone and Apple backup settings, Apple may include local App data in an Apple-managed device backup. Self Care does not control those backups or receive their contents.

Because the developer does not receive your check-in or Health information, the developer cannot retrieve, export, or delete that on-device information for you.

## 7. Security

Self Care relies on iOS app sandboxing, device protections, and HealthKit permission controls. No method of storage is completely risk-free, so you should protect access to your iPhone and carefully choose where you send exported files.

## 8. Wellbeing, not medical care

Self Care supports personal reflection and wellbeing. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition and is not a substitute for professional medical or mental-health care.

This policy may be updated if Self Care’s features or data practices change. The effective date and last-updated date at the top will identify the current version.
