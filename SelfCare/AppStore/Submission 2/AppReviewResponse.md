## 2. Purpose and target audience

Self Care is a private daily wellbeing reflection tool for people who want to notice how they are feeling and understand how everyday actions may relate to that experience. Users can record a check-in, track areas such as exercise, sleep, scrolling, food, and other personal categories, and review their choices over time. The app is intended for general wellbeing and self-reflection. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any medical condition and is not a substitute for professional medical care.

## 3. Setup and access

No account, registration, login, sample file, or test credentials are required.

To use the app:

1. Launch Self Care.
2. Complete the welcome flow if it is shown.
3. Select how you are feeling and choose any relevant wellbeing items.
4. Save the check-in.
5. Use Settings to manage categories, reminders, appearance, privacy information, and optional Apple Health access.

Apple Health access is optional. The app remains usable if Health permissions are declined. If enabled, the user controls which supported Health data types are shared with Self Care.

## 4. External services, tools, and platforms

- Apple HealthKit: optional access to supported Exercise Time and Sleep Analysis data, and optional export of State of Mind data when enabled by the user.
- Apple UserNotifications: optional local reminders scheduled on the device.
- Apple system frameworks and on-device storage: used for the app interface, local check-in records, and user preferences.

Self Care does not use third-party authentication, payment processors, advertising SDKs, analytics services, AI services, external data providers, or a remote user account system.

## 5. Regional behavior

Self Care's features and content function consistently across regions. The only differences may be Apple platform availability, HealthKit data-type availability, permission wording, or other operating-system and regional requirements controlled by Apple. No region-specific paid content or feature set is used.

## 6. Regulated services and third-party material

Self Care is not a regulated medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or emergency guidance. Apple Health access is optional and permission-controlled by the user.

The app does not provide regulated professional services or protected third-party material requiring special authorization. The app's branding, interface assets, and written content are supplied for this app by the developer, subject to the developer's ownership and licensing records.
